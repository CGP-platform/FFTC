The Reality

Agriculture is one of the most dangerous jobs in America - and help is often 20 to 60 minutes away.

The standard first-aid kit in most farm shops is built for splinters, not for what an auger, a PTO shaft, or a 1,200-lb animal actually does to a person. Most farm and ranch workers have never been trained in hemorrhage control, airway management, or shock recognition.

Being Part of the Solution

Two integrated products, one mission.

As a nonprofit, FFTC prices both to reach the farmhand and the smallholder - not just operations that can already afford private safety consulting.

KIT

The Farm First Aid Kit (FFAK)

An individual and site-level trauma kit designed around agricultural mechanisms of injury - amputation and crush injuries, large-animal trauma, falls, and severe bleeding far from definitive care. Offered in three tiers: Personal, Site, and Commercial/Ranch.

TRN

Mobile Trauma Training

Hands-on training delivered on-site - at the farm, the co-op, the county fair - via a mobile training unit, rather than requiring farmers to travel to a fixed classroom. Four course tiers, from a 2-hour Fundamentals course to a full Train-the-Trainer certification.

Evidence Based

Built on the same standards that train combat medics and trauma surgeons.

FFTC doesn't invent its own trauma doctrine - every technique traces back to a recognized, evidence-based framework, translated into plain-language instruction for a lay rural audience.

The FFAK: purpose-built for the farm, not a relabeled first-aid kit.

Every item traces back to a recognized trauma-care standard and a specific agricultural mechanism of injury - augers, PTO shafts, large-animal trauma, falls, and grain entrapment.

FFAK-Personal

Belt / truck kit, individual worker
  • CAT-style tourniquet + SOLO-T single-handed tourniquet
  • Compressed hemostatic gauze
  • Pressure/Israeli bandage + ACE wrap
  • Mini SAM splint
  • Vented chest seal
  • Trauma shears

FFAK-Site

Shop, barn, or cab kit - family operation
  • 3× tourniquets (CAT + SOLO-T), 2× hemostatic gauze
  • Burn dressing + SAM splint
  • ACE wraps ×2
  • Airway adjunct
  • Trauma shears + rigid, weatherproof case

FFAK-Commercial/Ranch

Crews & ranch vehicles, larger operations
  • 6× tourniquets (CAT + SOLO-T), extended hemostatic supply
  • Large-animal trauma guidance card
  • Spinal-precaution kit
  • Triage tags (multi-casualty)
  • ATV / truck-bed mountable case
The Goal: Each farm that graduates an FFTC Fundamentals or Advanced course takes home a free FFAK-Personal kit - training and equipment are delivered together, not sold separately.

Roadmap

Prove it in Wisconsin, then expand across the Upper Midwest.

✓ Done

Months 7–12

Expand across 8-12 target Wisconsin counties, working directly with local EMS in each. Lay the foundation for expansion beyond Wisconsin.

Scale to 20+ WI counties; first expansion county/region outside Wisconsin. Lay the foundation for international expansion.
